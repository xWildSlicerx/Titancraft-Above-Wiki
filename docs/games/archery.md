# Archery

- Need two free inventory slots, recommended to have 2 hotbar slots free
- Use crossbow
	- Single click to load, single click to shoot
	- the supplied arrow has special metadata on it.
 	- Recommended to not have other arrows in hotbar 
- Shoot target blocks
	- Only counts if you hit close to the bullseye
	- Success will play a Note block sound
   	- Fail will play a Breeze charge "poof"
- In Multiplayer, who ever hits the most wins
	- No rewards 
- In Single Player
	- 40-59 points grants 8 Splinters
	- 60+ points grants 12 Splinters
- Cave Entrance is on Spruce Island
  	- Coords to cave entrance near Coarse Dirt portal: 36, 91, -418
